The interactive interface: launching it, the anatomy of the three columns,
how a repository's resources are loaded on demand, navigation, sorting and
filtering, selection, confirmation, progress and errors, the Billing tab,
behaviour on narrow terminals, and the complete keyboard-shortcut table. It
will also explain every symbol the interface uses — `⛑`, `•`, `⚑`, `⚠`, `✓`
and `✗` — in one place.
